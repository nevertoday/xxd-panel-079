# XXD Panel 079 · Faithful English Reading Translation

Panel 079 turns each uploaded photograph into a separate 3:4 poster with a preserved reality view above and an unfinished pen-and-wash sketch below. The lower half extracts only the most recognisable subject, contour, pose, space, and narrative relationship.

Strong straight perspective lines, angular contours, geometric axes, and planar simplification establish a rational scaffold. Free organic curves, searching marks, broken lines, slight offsets, and unresolved edges interrupt that order with a hand-drawn quality.

Use only a few transparent watercolour accents derived from the source photograph's recognisable colours. Allow slight overrun, watermarks, grain, and uneven coverage; keep broad near-white natural paper visible so ink, wash, and paper form a clear hierarchy. Remove complex background information and let negative space carry air, distance, light, and pause.

Typography is sparse and meaningful, using fine editorial type with slight handmade registration shifts. Let it follow perspective lines, geometric axes, subject edges, or open space rather than forming a conventional title-and-body menu. The result should resemble an art-directed architectural travel sketchbook, independent visual journal, or field-observation manual. Avoid meticulous realism, filled backgrounds, chaotic doodling, immaculate technical drafting, thick black contours, photo-outline filters, cartoons, 3D rendering, and commercial-template aesthetics.
